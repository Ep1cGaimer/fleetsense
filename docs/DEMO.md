# Evidence console runbook

Start the local server with `.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m app.server`. The evidence console reads only locked result exports; no fitting or simulation happens in HTTP requests.

Show the verdict, then fleet replay. Play the daily sequence and inspect a planned start, an unavailable aircraft, and the independent-checker badge. An engine serial links to its model-evidence prefix; this is not diagnosis or live sensor telemetry.

Compare policy heatmaps and the reporting-only frontier. Losing cells remain visible. Inspect calibration at the horizons actually used and the decision-point, near-threshold and subgroup rows. Then show leakage, poisoned-future, mutation and scale audits, and finish with the generated claim ledger.

Capture, narration, video, slide and PDF tooling are owned by the main implementation. They must consume `results/summary.json` and may not replace the selected verdict.
